About RustyScope

The RustyScope is an open-source telescope project designed for amateur astronomers and hobbyists. Our goal is to make astronomy accessible to everyone with affordable, customizable telescope solutions.

Built with a combination of modern technology and classic design principles, RustyScope offers exceptional performance without the premium price tag.

Key Features

High-Quality Optics

Premium lenses and mirrors for crisp, clear views of celestial objects.

Modular Design

Customizable components to suit your specific astronomical interests.

Open-Source Software

Community-developed software for tracking and image processing.
